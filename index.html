Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

In short: The Parkrun Barcode app is designed to work without collecting or transmitting your personal information. Your parkrun ID is stored locally on your Garmin device so that your barcode can be displayed when you need it.

1. About This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how the Parkrun Barcode app ("the App") handles information when you use the App on a compatible Garmin device.

We respect your privacy and aim to collect and process as little information as possible. The App is designed primarily as an offline utility for displaying a user's parkrun barcode.

2. Information We Process

The App allows you to enter your parkrun ID. This ID is used to generate and display your barcode on your Garmin device.

The parkrun ID you enter is stored locally on your Garmin device using the device's application storage.

The App does not intentionally collect information such as:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your telephone number
  • Your postal address
  • Your precise location
  • Your health information
  • Your contacts
  • Your photographs or media
  • Your account passwords
  • Your payment information

3. How Your Parkrun ID Is Used

Your parkrun ID is used solely to generate the barcode that is displayed by the App.

The ID is stored locally on your Garmin device so that you do not have to enter it every time you open the App.

The App does not intentionally send your parkrun ID to the developer or to a server operated by the developer.

4. Data Transmission

The App does not have a developer-operated online account system or backend server for storing your parkrun ID.

Your entered parkrun ID remains on your Garmin device for use by the App.

The App does not intentionally transmit your entered parkrun ID to the developer.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or otherwise provide your personal information to third parties.

Because the App does not intentionally collect personal information through a developer-operated service, there is no developer database of users' parkrun IDs.

6. Data Retention

Your parkrun ID is retained locally on your Garmin device until you remove it, replace it, or uninstall the App, subject to how your Garmin device manages application data.

The developer does not maintain a separate copy of your parkrun ID.

7. Deleting Your Data

Because your parkrun ID is stored locally on your device, you can remove the data by using the App's available reset functionality or by removing the App from your Garmin device, where applicable.

If you have questions about removing locally stored data, you can contact us using the details below.

8. Device and Garmin Services

The App operates within the Garmin Connect IQ platform and runs on compatible Garmin devices.

Garmin and Garmin's services may process information independently of this App. Such processing is controlled by Garmin and is subject to Garmin's own privacy policies and terms.

This Privacy Policy applies only to the Parkrun Barcode App and does not describe Garmin's separate processing of information.

9. Third-Party Services

The App does not intentionally integrate third-party advertising, analytics, tracking, or user-profiling services.

The App does not intentionally use your parkrun ID for advertising or marketing purposes.

10. Security

We take reasonable steps to avoid unnecessary collection and processing of personal information.

Because the parkrun ID is stored locally on the Garmin device rather than in a developer-operated database, the App does not maintain a central database containing users' parkrun IDs.

You should nevertheless protect access to your Garmin device and keep your device software up to date.

11. Children's Privacy

The App does not intentionally collect personal information from children or create user profiles.

As the App is designed to minimise data collection, no developer-operated account or personal-information database is required to use the App.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on where you live and the laws that apply to you, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including rights to:

  • Request information about how your data is used
  • Request access to personal information held about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of personal information
  • Object to certain processing
  • Request restriction of certain processing

Because the App is designed not to maintain a developer database of users' parkrun IDs, there may be little or no personal information held by the developer in relation to your use of the App.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time if the App's functionality, data practices, or applicable legal requirements change.

When this policy is updated, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be changed.

We recommend checking this page periodically for the latest version.

14. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the App's privacy practices, please contact us.

Developer: Croftonix
Email: croftonixstudios@outlook.com

15. Complaints

If you are in the United Kingdom and believe your personal information has been handled in a way that breaches data protection law, you can raise your concerns with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

You can find information about making a complaint on the ICO's official website.
